💡 Quick pitch: "Hello everyone! Today I want to showcase VexorUX—a modern, full-stack digital experience platform engineered with interactive 3D WebGL visuals, real-time pricing estimation, and production-grade architecture."
The Motivation

The Challenge with Modern Websites

Most agency and business websites suffer from 3 critical issues:

😴 Static & Generic

Flat, static templates with zero visual excitement that fail to keep high-value visitors engaged.

❓ Opaque Pricing

Visitors have to wait days for sales calls just to understand what a digital project will cost.

📉 Poor Conversion

Boring lead forms with poor UX that lead to high bounce rates and lost client opportunities.

💡 What to say: "Most digital agency websites look the same—flat templates where visitors bounce in seconds, and clients never know pricing upfront. VexorUX was built to solve this."
The Solution

Enter VexorUX

A cutting-edge platform combining immersive visuals with instant utility.

⚡ Immersive 3D Experience

Built-in interactive WebGL canvas that reacts smoothly to user mouse movement and device capabilities.

💰 Live Cost Calculator

Clients configure project scope, timeline, and features to receive immediate, transparent cost estimates.

⌘ Quick Actions Palette (Ctrl+K)

Power-user command dialog inspired by macOS Spotlight for instant keyboard navigation.

🛡️ End-to-End Inquiry Pipeline

Validated booking modal connected directly to an Express & MongoDB backend.

💡 What to say: "VexorUX replaces boring pages with an interactive experience: Three.js 3D visuals, a transparent project cost calculator, and quick keyboard navigation."
Architecture

The Technology Stack

Built with performance, modern standards, and clean modularity in mind.

🎨 Frontend

HTML5, Modern CSS3 variables, Glassmorphism, Vanilla JS (ES6+). Zero bloat, fast load times.

🎮 3D Graphics

Three.js running on WebGL with custom particle fields, wireframe mesh, and frame throttles.

⚙️ Backend & Database

Node.js + Express REST API backed by MongoDB with schema validation via Mongoose.

Three.js JavaScript (ES6+) Node.js Express.js MongoDB Mongoose CSS Glassmorphism
💡 What to say: "Instead of heavy bloated frameworks, the frontend is built lightweight with vanilla JavaScript and Three.js for 60fps performance, paired with an Express/MongoDB backend."
Features

Engineering Highlights

🎯 3D Scene with Adaptive FPS

The Three.js scene dynamically monitors performance and gracefully optimizes rendering for mobile or low-power devices.

📊 Real-time Price Estimation

Dynamic JavaScript calculator engine computing base cost, add-ons (animations, e-commerce, custom 3D), and timelines on the fly.

📋 Command Palette Navigation

Accessible via Ctrl+K or ⌘K, allowing visitors to jump to sections, open calculators, or contact instantly.

📨 Seamless Lead Capture

Full inquiry schema tracking client name, email, project type, timeline, and estimated budget into MongoDB.

💡 What to say: "The 3D canvas is GPU-accelerated and adapts to device performance. The cost calculator gives clients real-time estimates, and Ctrl+K lets keyboard power-users navigate seamlessly."
